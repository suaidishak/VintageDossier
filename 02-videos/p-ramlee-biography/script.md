# Script: P. Ramlee - The Boy From Penang Who Became a Legend
Format 9:16 · target 2:00 · ~300 words (±5 %) · chapter headers are NOT read aloud

## Chapter 1 - The Penang Boy
May 1973. Malaysia's greatest entertainer dies of a heart attack. He is just forty-four.
In his final years, almost no one wanted his films.
His name is P. Ramlee. Today, an entire nation calls him a legend.
He was born Teuku Zakaria, on the 22nd of March 1929, in George Town, Penang.
His father was a sailor from Aceh, in Sumatra.
When Japan occupied Malaya, his schooling stopped.
But at a Japanese naval school, he learned violin, piano, and song.
After the war, he played in village bands.
In 1947, he won Penang Radio's singing contest.

## Chapter 2 - The Golden Age
In 1948, a film director heard him sing his own song, Azizah.
He took the young singer south, to the Shaw Brothers studio in Singapore.
Ramlee started as a backing singer. Then he played a villain. Soon he was the star.
He acted, he sang, he composed, and then he directed.
Hang Tuah won an Asian film award for its music.
Anakku Sazali made him Best Actor in Tokyo.
Bujang Lapok made a whole region laugh.
In all, he acted in sixty-six films, and directed thirty-five.

## Chapter 3 - The Legend
In 1964, he moved to a new studio in Kuala Lumpur.
But tastes were changing, and the film industry was in trouble.
His new films struggled, and the crowds moved on.
On the 29th of May 1973, his heart gave out.
Then Malaysia remembered.
His songs and films found a new generation.
In 1990, he was honoured, after his death, with the title Tan Sri.
His childhood home in Penang is now a museum.
The boy from Penang never stopped singing. A nation simply learned to listen.
