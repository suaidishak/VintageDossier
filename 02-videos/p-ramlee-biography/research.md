# Research: Tan Sri P. Ramlee - biography
Format: assumed VERTICAL 9:16, 1080x1920 (as previous videos) unless the viewer says otherwise.

## Summary
P. Ramlee was born Teuku Zakaria bin Teuku Nyak Puteh on 22 March 1929 in George Town, Penang; his father was a sailor from
Aceh (Sumatra), his mother Penang-born. ("P." comes from his father's name, Puteh - verify wording.)
His schooling (Kampung Jawa Malay School, Francis Light, Penang Free School) was interrupted by the Japanese occupation
(1942-45); at a Japanese naval school he learned music (violin, piano, ukulele) and Japanese songs.
He played in village bands, entered Penang Radio singing contests (third, then runner-up, then winner in 1947).
In 1948 Shaw Brothers' director B.S. Rajhans heard him sing his own song "Azizah" and took him to Malay Film Productions
in Singapore - first as a backing singer, then a villain in "Cinta" (1948), then the leading man.
In Singapore (1948-1964) he became the golden age of Malay cinema: actor, director, composer, singer.
Awards: Best Musical Score for "Hang Tuah" (Asian Film Festival 1956), Best Actor for "Anakku Sazali" (Tokyo 1957),
Best Comedy for "Pendekar Bujang Lapok" (1959). Classics: Bujang Lapok series, Ibu Mertuaku (1962), Seniman Bujang Lapok.
In 1964 he moved to Kuala Lumpur (Merdeka Studio). Audiences and the industry changed; his later films and records struggled.
He died of a heart attack on 29 May 1973, aged 44, largely forgotten and in financial difficulty.
Today he is Malaysia's greatest cultural icon: posthumous Tan Sri (6 June 1990), his birth house is a museum, a road bears his name.

## Numbers
- Films acted: 66; directed: 35 (NLB Infopedia)
- Songs: composed ~250 (Infopedia) / recorded 359 (Wikipedia) / wrote 500+ (BiblioAsia) - sources differ
- Age at death: 44
- Visitors to his two memorials: 100,000+ a year (Malay Mail 2026)

## Disputed / uncertain
- Where he was discovered: keroncong show in Penang (NLB/Roots) vs Radio Malaya contest in Bukit Mertajam (Wikipedia).
- Song counts vary widely (250 / 359 / 500+).
- Why his career declined after 1964: changing tastes, industry and union troubles after Singapore's separation,
  claims of sabotage by rivals - several factors, not one documented cause.
- Saloma marriage year (1961) and museum opening year (1990/1992) to verify.
