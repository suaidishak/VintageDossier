# Research: Tan Sri Syed Mokhtar Albukhary - biography
Format: assumed VERTICAL 9:16, 1080x1920 (same as the previous video) unless the viewer says otherwise.

## Summary
Syed Mokhtar Albukhary was born on 12 December 1951 in Kampung Hutan Keriang, Alor Setar, Kedah, third child of Syed Nor
and Sharifah Nor; the family traces its roots to Hadhramaut, Yemen (father a trader who settled in Kedah in the 1940s).
He grew up poor; as a schoolboy he helped in his father's cattle business, which collapsed after a foot-and-mouth outbreak.
He left school early (St. Michael's Institution, Alor Setar) and started trading rice and running lorries, helped by a MARA loan
and Bumiputera transport permits ("I could afford only two lorries, but was issued four permits").
In the 1990s, during Mahathir's privatisation drive, he built a conglomerate: MMC Corporation (ports incl. Tanjung Pelepas,
Johor Port; Malakoff power; Senai airport), DRB-Hicom (Proton until Geely took 49.9 % in 2017; Pos Malaysia; Bank Muamalat)
and Bernas, the national rice importer.
He is famously publicity-shy and gives few interviews. Forbes lists him among Malaysia's richest (about US$2.2-2.4 bn in 2025;
US$3.5 bn in 2026 per Forbes via Wikipedia).
Through the Albukhary Foundation he funds education, mosques, and the Islamic Arts Museum; Albukhary International University
(Alor Setar, 2010) gives free education to poor students. Forbes Asia "Hero of Philanthropy" 2014.
Critics say his rise relied on ties to Mahathir and access to privatised national assets; the Bernas rice import monopoly is a
recurring political issue (PM Anwar said in 2022 he "reprimanded" him over it). Mahathir says he turned around ailing firms.
