# Script: Anwar Ibrahim - From Prisoner to Prime Minister
Format 9:16 · target 2:00 · ~300 words (±5 %) · chapter headers are NOT read aloud

## Chapter 1 - The Heir
September 1998. Malaysia's former Deputy Prime Minister walks into court with a swollen black eye.
Weeks earlier, he was the man expected to run the country.
His name is Anwar Ibrahim. And his fall was only the beginning.

He was born on the 10th of August 1947, in a small village in Penang.
As a student, he led Muslim youth groups and became a fierce voice for the poor.
In 1974, he stood with students protesting hunger among rubber tappers in Baling.
He was detained without trial for twenty months.
Then, in 1982, a surprise. Prime Minister Mahathir Mohamad invited him into the ruling party, UMNO.
Anwar rose fast. Finance Minister in 1991. Deputy Prime Minister in 1993.
Everyone saw him as Mahathir's heir.

## Chapter 2 - The Fall
Then the Asian financial crisis hit, and the two men split.
On the 2nd of September 1998, Mahathir sacked him.
Anwar took his fight to the streets. The crowds called it Reformasi.
Weeks later, he was arrested and beaten in police custody.
Courts convicted him of corruption, then sodomy.
He called the charges political. So did international rights groups.
In 2004, the top court overturned the sodomy verdict, and he walked free after six years.
In 2008, he won back his old seat and led the opposition.
But in 2015, a second sodomy conviction sent him back to prison.

## Chapter 3 - The Return
Then, in May 2018, a twist.
His coalition won the election, allied with his old rival, Mahathir.
The King granted Anwar a full pardon.
Four years later, a new election left no party with a majority.
On the 24th of November 2022, Anwar Ibrahim was sworn in as Malaysia's tenth Prime Minister.
He was seventy-five years old.
Twenty-four years after the black eye, the prisoner had become the Prime Minister.
