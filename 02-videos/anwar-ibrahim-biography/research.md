# Research: Anwar Ibrahim - biography (Malaysia's 10th Prime Minister)
Format decision (viewer, 2026-11-25): VERTICAL 9:16, 1080x1920, 30 fps (overrides MASTER-PROMPT 16:9 default).

## Summary
Anwar bin Ibrahim was born on 10 August 1947 in Cherok Tok Kun, Bukit Mertajam, Penang.
As a student leader he led Muslim student unions and co-founded ABIM, the Muslim Youth Movement of Malaysia.
In 1974 he led student protests over rural poverty in Baling, Kedah, and was detained without trial under the ISA (~20 months).
In 1982 he surprised many by joining UMNO at Mahathir Mohamad's invitation instead of the Islamist party PAS.
He rose fast: Finance Minister (1991) and Deputy Prime Minister (Dec 1993), seen as Mahathir's heir.
During the 1997-98 Asian financial crisis the two clashed; on 2 Sep 1998 Mahathir sacked him.
He launched the Reformasi movement, was arrested on 20 Sep 1998 and beaten in custody (the "black eye").
He was convicted of corruption (1999, 6 yrs) and sodomy (2000, 9 yrs); the sodomy verdict was overturned on 2 Sep 2004.
A second sodomy case (2008) ended in acquittal (2012), then conviction (2014), upheld Feb 2015: back to prison.
In May 2018 his coalition, now allied with Mahathir, won; the King granted a full pardon on 16 May 2018.
After the 2020 "Sheraton Move" collapse and a hung parliament in Nov 2022, he was sworn in as the 10th PM on 24 Nov 2022.
Supporters call the cases political persecution (Amnesty: prisoner of conscience); the government of the day said the courts decided.

  politically motivated. Script must state both: "courts convicted him - he and rights groups said it was political".

- ABIM founding: 1971 vs 1972.
